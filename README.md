#  Digital Pusher

### AI-Powered Digital Safety and Scam Awareness

Digital Pusher is an AI-powered solution designed to help people identify potential online scams, misleading messages, and manipulative digital practices. It aims to make digital interactions safer by explaining suspicious content in simple, understandable language and helping users make informed decisions.

##  Problem Statement

People receive suspicious messages, fraudulent links, fake offers, and misleading online content every day. Many users struggle to identify these threats, which can lead to financial losses, privacy risks, and compromised personal information.

Traditional awareness methods may not be enough to help users recognize new and evolving forms of digital deception.

##  Our Solution

Digital Pusher aims to use Artificial Intelligence to analyze potentially suspicious digital content, highlight warning signs, and explain why a message or online interaction may be risky.

Our goal is not just to flag suspicious content, but also to **educate users and encourage safer digital behavior.**

##  Key Features

-  **Scam Detection:** Analyze messages and content for potential signs of fraud.
-  **AI-Powered Analysis:** Use AI to identify suspicious patterns and manipulative language.
-  **Simple Explanations:** Explain potential risks in easy-to-understand language.
-  **Accessible Design:** Aim to make digital safety assistance accessible to a wide range of users.
-  **Digital Awareness:** Help users recognize common scam techniques and make informed decisions.
-  **Privacy Awareness:** Encourage users to avoid sharing sensitive information or clicking unverified links.

##  How It Works

1. **Input:** The user submits a suspicious message, link, or supported digital content.
2. **Analysis:** The system analyzes the submitted content using AI.
3. **Risk Identification:** Potential warning signs, suspicious language, or manipulative tactics are highlighted.
4. **Explanation:** The user receives a clear explanation of the possible risks.
5. **Safer Action:** The user gets guidance on how to proceed cautiously.

##  Proposed Technology Stack

The final technology stack depends on the implementation.

- **AI:** Google Gemini
- **Frontend:** Typescript 
- **Backend:** FastAPI
- **Database:** MongoDB

##  Target Audience

- Older adults and senior citizens who may need additional support identifying online scams
- Students and young internet users
- People who frequently use digital payment services
- Individuals who receive suspicious messages or online offers
- Users who want to improve their digital safety awareness

##  Expected Impact

Digital Pusher aims to:

- Improve awareness of online scams and deceptive practices.
- Help users understand potential digital threats.
- Encourage responsible online behavior.
- Make AI-assisted digital safety guidance easier to access.

##  Future Enhancements

- Multilingual explanations for regional language users.
- Browser extension for analyzing suspicious webpages.
